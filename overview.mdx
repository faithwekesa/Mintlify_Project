## What Ishuko does

Ishuko is an agri-tech mobile app that bridges the gap between cooperatives selling maize produce and wholesale buyers who need it. Cooperatives get quality assessment grades for their produce by taking photos in the app. Computer vision AI grades the maize into one of four categories:

| Grade | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A | Excellent |
| B | Good |
| C | Average |
| D | Poor |

A Quality Assessment Report is generated for the cooperative to market their produce on the integrated marketplace. Buyers place and pay for orders via the mobile app, built with Flutter.

## Problem statement

<Tip>
  Despite digital platforms providing market access for smallholder farmers in Zambia, poor produce quality still limits access to buyers, leading to exploitation by middlemen. Commercial buyers remain reluctant to source directly from small-scale farmers due to a lack of quality assurance.
</Tip>

## Key features

Ishuko's core differentiators are covered in full on  [Core Features](/core-features) : AI computer-vision grading, weakest-link quality thresholds, a live-market-price pricing engine, escrow-backed transactions, OTP-verified pickup, and a grade-first marketplace.

## Workflow overview

A single transaction flows through five stages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grade">
    A cooperative manager captures exactly 3 photos of a produce sample; the AI pipeline assigns a grade (A–D) and generates a Quality Assessment Report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List">
    The graded batch is priced automatically (live market price × grade modifier) and published to the marketplace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order">
    A wholesale buyer browses the marketplace, places an order for a full listed quantity, and selects a pickup date within 7 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay into escrow">
    The buyer deposits payment, held in escrow rather than paid directly to the cooperative; the buyer receives the depot location and a 6-digit pickup code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify & settle">
    At the depot, the buyer hands the cooperative manager the code; a successful match instantly releases escrowed funds and closes the order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Architecture](/architecture) for the same journey traced through system components.

## Product objectives

- **Market access** — automatically allocating market price based on assigned grade, so cooperatives reach buyers willing to pay fairly.
- **Subjective grading** — replaced with an AI grading system based on image analysis, removing arbitrary downgrading.
- **Cooperative invisibility** — addressed through an AI-generated quality assurance grade that builds trust.

| Feature | Description |

| Direct-to-Consumer Marketplace | Retail customers buying small quantities directly from farmers |
| Logistics Fleet Management | A transport network managing produce movement from farm → depot → buyer |

## Assumptions

- A photographic sample is a statistically accurate representation of the entire aggregated batch, allowing the AI to assign a reliable grade.
- Objective AI grading bridges the trust gap with wholesalers, eliminating inspection delays and ensuring immediate payment to the cooperative.
- Buyers are willing to pay for produce in full before pickup.
- Most cooperatives and buyers use mobile money options like Airtel and Zamtel.
- Buyers can select a later pickup date.

## Dependencies

- **Airtel Africa Developer Portal** (Airtel Money Zambia) — secure authentication handshakes and C2B/B2B/Transaction Refund API access
- **Production SSL & Routing Layer** (HTTPS/TLS) — for asynchronous transaction confirmations and telecom webhooks
- **Camera Hardware Bridge Module** — locks image resolution parameters, blocks gallery uploads
- **Image Compression Unit** — compresses images to minimize data usage during transmission
- **Cryptographic Token Library** — generates single-use 6-digit codes
- **YOLOv8 Framework** — scans the image and outputs a grade
- **OpenCV** — handles preprocessing (spatial transforms, exposure normalization, contrast enhancement) before quality checking
- **Live Market Data** (UN WFP Humanitarian Data Exchange) — real-time pricing feed
